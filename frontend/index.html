How well it works

Evaluated on a balanced sample of 15,000 Amazon food reviews (5,000 per class), where 1-2 stars are negative, 3 stars neutral and 4-5 stars positive.

81%Correct on positive and negative reviews
61%Three-class accuracy
20%Neutral recall

The model separates clearly positive and negative reviews well. Three-star reviews are the hard case: they often mix praise and complaints, and the model usually assigns them to one side.
